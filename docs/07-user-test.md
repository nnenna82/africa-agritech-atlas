# Phase 2 test: 5 people, 5 tasks

Phase 2 is done when at least 4 of 5 people finish their task in under 5 minutes on the live site.

## How to run it
1. Give each person the live link on **their own phone**. Use a fresh browser (or a private window) so the bar starts empty.
2. Read them their one task. Do not show them where to click.
3. Start a timer. Stay quiet. Note where they hesitate.
4. Stop the timer when they say the answer out loud. Then ask the two questions at the bottom.

## The tasks (one per audience)

| # | Who | Task to read out | They pass if they… |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Student or newcomer | "You are new to African farming. Find out why cocoa farmers get so little of a chocolate bar's price, then name one person you could learn from in Ghana." | Open the Cocoa crop journey, then find a Ghana person (People page, or For you with Ghana picked). |
| 2 | Founder or builder | "You want to build something for storing food after harvest in Nigeria. Name one documented problem and one startup that already tried." | Pick Founder + Nigeria + storage & cold chain in the bar and read the For you panel, or use the Gap map. |
| 3 | Investor or funder | "Find one active startup in East Africa you might fund, and one development lender that works there." | Pick Investor + East Africa and read the For you panel. |
| 4 | Policy professional | "Find which group Kenya is in, and Kenya's weakest area." | Open Kenya from the map or the bar; read the color group and the pillar list. |
| 5 | Researcher | "Find the latest news story about West Africa and say why it matters." | Pick West Africa in the bar and open News (or read Latest news on Home). |
