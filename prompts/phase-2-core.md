# Phase 2 prompts: the core promise (finishes version 1)

Do one per session if you like. Start each session with:
```
Read CLAUDE.md and docs/02-build-plan.md. We're on Phase 2: <feature>.
```

1. **The bar:** `Build the bar from docs/01-product-spec.md: I am a, Region, Country (locked until a region is picked), Interested in, and Clear. Remember choices in the visitor's browser only. Make Region and Country filter every list.`
2. **Gap map:** `Build /gaps with cards and filters as in the spec (screenshot 05). Connect it to the bar.`
3. **People:** `Build /people (screenshot 06). Show the "being checked" note for needs_review records. Never show personal contact details.`
4. **For you and interest panels on Home:** `Add the "For you" panel (by role) and the interest panel (by part of the chain) to Home, and the "My interest" map coloring. Use the prototype functions forYou and segPrimer in prototype/atlas-prototype.html as reference.`
5. **Crop Journeys:** `Add the gold Crop Journeys menu button, the four-crop band at the top of Home, and crop cards on country pages, all linking to the Crop Journeys page (link in prototype/README.md).`
6. **News:** `Build /news (screenshot 07) and the latest three stories on Home, filtered by the bar.`
7. **Test:** `Help me prepare a 5-person test: give me 5 tasks, one per audience, that each should take under 5 minutes, and a simple sheet to record results.`
